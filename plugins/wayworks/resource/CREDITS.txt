Wayworks artwork
===============

The worker model and the worker, planner and bus-stop pixel-art textures are
original procedural artwork created for this plugin. They are dedicated to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Texture source: ../generate-assets.ts in the Wayworks source folder.
No third-party artwork or sound recordings are included.
Minecraft supplies its own built-in block geometry and rendering materials;
no Minecraft asset files have been copied into this pack.
